Why it’s different

It hands back an answer you can check.

A solver is one of the few tools whose output you can’t eyeball. You asked because you didn’t know the answer. So Plumline doesn’t ask for trust. It shows what it understood, verifies the result against your own formulas, and tells you which limit is holding you back.

01

It reads your model

No blank form to fill. Plumline finds the goal, the quantities and the limits in the sheet you already have, named in your own words.

02

It verifies the answer

It writes the solution back and lets the sheet recalculate, so you see both numbers agree before you rely on it.

03

It shows what one more is worth

For every limit that holds you back, its estimated one-unit impact, often called a shadow price: how much more you'd gain from one more unit, while the model stays the same. A sensitivity-style insight, explained in plain language.

See it work

From a spreadsheet to a verified answer.

You write the problem the way you already write a spreadsheet: labels in the first column, numbers in the cells, ordinary formulas. No special syntax, no new language. Here is a small one, a workshop deciding how many chairs and tables to make.

What you type (ordinary spreadsheet formulas)

Questions

Before you try it.

Do I need to install anything?

For the online solver, no. It runs in your browser at plumline.online. A Google Sheets add-on is on the way and will be a free, one-time install from the Workspace Marketplace once it's approved.

How do I write the model?

With ordinary spreadsheet formulas. Put your options in rows, a cell that totals what you care about (the goal), and any limits as rows with an operator and a number. Plumline reads it from there, no special syntax.

What kinds of problems does it solve?

Linear and integer problems: production mix, purchasing, blending, staffing, transport, budget splits. It does not solve genuinely non-linear ones, such as a quantity multiplied by another quantity, and it tells you if it meets one.

Are my numbers safe?

Yes. The online solver runs entirely in your browser and uploads nothing. The add-on sends no spreadsheet data to Plumline or third parties; its processing takes place within Google's infrastructure under your own account. There is no Plumline account to create and no tracking.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Both the online solver and the add-on are free.
