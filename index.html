Why it's different

It hands back an answer you can check.

A solver is one of the few tools whose output you can't eyeball. You asked because you didn't know the answer. So Plumline doesn't ask for trust. It shows what it understood, proves the result against your own formulas, and tells you which limit is holding you back.

It reads your model

No blank form to fill. Plumline finds the goal, the quantities and the limits in the sheet you already have, named in your own words.

It proves the answer

It writes the solution back and lets the sheet recalculate, so you see both numbers agree before you rely on it.

It shows what one more is worth

For every limit that holds you back, the shadow price: how much more you'd gain from one more unit. The report the paid tools charge for.
