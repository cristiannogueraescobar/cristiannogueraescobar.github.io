Plumline

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 July 2026.

The short version

Plumline does not collect, transmit, sell or store your data anywhere outside the spreadsheet you are using it in. There is no Plumline server, no account, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains that in full.

What Plumline accesses

When you open the Plumline panel in a spreadsheet, it reads the values and formulas of that spreadsheet in order to work out the model and solve it. It reads nothing until you open the panel, and it reads nothing outside the spreadsheet the add-on is installed in.

Plumline holds two authorisations from your Google Account:

  • spreadsheets.currentonly — access limited to the spreadsheet the add-on is installed in. It cannot reach any other file in your Google Drive.
  • script.container.ui — permission to display the side panel.

It requests no access to Gmail, Google Drive at large, Calendar, Contacts or your profile information, and it makes no requests to external services.

What Plumline stores, and where

So that you do not have to define your model again every time, Plumline saves the definition of it — which cells are the quantities, which cell is the total, and the limits with their values — using the Google Apps Script document properties of that same spreadsheet. It also keeps a copy of the previous values of the quantity cells, so that the last write can be undone.

This information stays attached to your spreadsheet, inside Google's infrastructure. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you use "forget this model" in the panel, when you uninstall the add-on, or when the spreadsheet is deleted.

What Plumline does not do

  • It does not transmit your spreadsheet contents anywhere.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not create an account or ask for an email address.
  • It does not use your data to train any model.
  • It does not share or sell data, because it holds none to share or sell.

This website has no analytics either, and loads no fonts, scripts or images from third parties.

Where the processing happens

Plumline runs as a Google Apps Script bound to your spreadsheet, so the reading, the calculation and the writing all happen within Google's infrastructure, under your own Google Account. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's privacy policy. No third party is involved.

Limited use

Plumline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used solely to provide the feature you invoked, is not transferred to others, and is not used for advertising or for any purpose other than solving the model in front of you.

Your control

You can revoke Plumline's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by uninstalling the add-on. Because no data leaves your spreadsheet, there is nothing to request or delete from us.

Changes and contact
