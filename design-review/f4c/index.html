The Plumline motion system.

Every specimen below is driven by the single canonical motion source in src/shared/motion/, layered on the F4b design system in src/shared/design-system/. This is a laboratory, not a landing page: it exists to prove the motion primitives are reusable, accessible, progressively enhanced and easy to clean up before any public page adopts them. Calm while working, precise while changing, deliberate while explaining.

5  Product sequence

A demonstration of the production-plan flow. Not the engine. Without JS, all five steps are readable as a list. With JavaScript and normal motion, one step shows at a time with a pauseable demonstration; reduced motion disables autoplay. Autoplay stops at the last step and never loops. This is the reusable primitive F9 will consume.
