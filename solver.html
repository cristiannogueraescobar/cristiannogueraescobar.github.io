The add-on How to use

Free online solver

Optimise a spreadsheet without leaving your browser.

Type your problem into the grid the way you would in any spreadsheet. Plumline reads it, finds the best allocation, and proves the answer. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is nothing to install.

Start from an example

Yellow cells are the quantities to decide. Put an operator (≤, ≥, =) and a number to the right of a limit. Edit anything and solve again.

How it reads your grid

Plumline looks for one cell that totals the others: that becomes the goal. The cells that total feeds into, and that are currently empty or zero, become the quantities it can change. Any row with a comparison operator and a number becomes a limit. It uses the labels in your first column to name everything, so the answer reads in plain words.

It solves linear and integer problems: production, purchasing, blending, staffing, transport, budgets. It does not solve genuinely non-linear ones, such as a quantity multiplied by another quantity, and it will point at the cell if it meets one.
